Projects

This section lists some of my past and current projects both from my academic career and personnal life.

Academic projects

Sagemath

In 2019, I had the opportunity to participate to the SageMath project, an open-source python-based formal calculus program. I contributed to the SageManifold module, the first symbolic tool to allow for arbitrary (sub-)manifold manipulation. It is particularly useful for computation in the field of General Relativity.

Black Hole Trajectory Simulator

This project is the result of having implemented geodesic computation on arbitrary topologies in Sagemath. It is now very easy to simulate the propagation of light backwards in spacetime around a black hole. After some number-crunching, a complete image of an acretion disk can be simulated. My step-by-step notebook can be found here.

HighPT

HighPT is a Mathematica package for the analysis of high-energy semileptonic transition at hadron colliders. This program was concieved during my PhD thesis as a way for theorist to quickly estimate the likelyhood of a theory beyond the Standard Model of particle physics, in light of the most recent LHC results. HighPT is hosted and maintained on github.

Projets Personnels

Timetable Solver

Timetables can be a sensitive topic for teachers. In June 2025, I privately help a middle school in France with elaborating schedules for the following school year. Professional tools are available, but often make it very hard to include new constraints, even if they seem intuitive at first glance. Constraints such as "these two classes must have French lesson at the same time" can quicly become impossible to track by hand.

This kind of constraints, which are more and more required due to new teaching practices, often result in other constraints being neglected, such as professor preferences.

This program can enumerate solutions without compromise, neither for the professors nor students. It makes it very easy to add new constraints. Based on the CP_SAT solver of OR-Tools, it is able to find an optimal solution for an entire school of 27 teachers and 50 classes, while respecting all personnal preferences and without any hole in the professor or student schedules.
